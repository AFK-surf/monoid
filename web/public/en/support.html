Support

Help and contact information for still.fail

If you need help with still.fail, run into a bug, or have a question about your account, reach us through the channels below.

1. Contact us

  • Email: hi@still.fail
  • Bug reports and feature requests: GitHub Issues
  • In-app feedback: Open Settings › Feedback in the app to send a report with diagnostic details.
  • Security issues: Please report vulnerabilities privately following SECURITY.md.

2. Common help

  • Connecting a station: Install and sign in to a station on your macOS, Linux, or Windows computer, then start and follow coding agent chats from your phone or browser.
  • Account and safe mode: Manage your signed-in sessions and trusted devices under Settings › Account in the app.
  • Deleting your account: Open Settings › Account in the app and choose “Delete account” to remove your account and cloud records immediately.

3. Operator and legal

The Service is operated by an individual developer based in mainland China. For more details, see our Privacy policy, Terms of service, and Trademark policy.
